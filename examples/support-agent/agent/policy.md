Larkspur Mobile customer policy (effective January 2026). Larkspur Mobile is a fictional company used for this demo.

1. Device returns: within 14 days of delivery, in original condition, the customer gets the purchase price back minus a $35 restocking fee. After 14 days, devices cannot be returned or refunded.
2. Outage credits: for a service outage longer than 24 consecutive hours, the customer gets a $5 credit per full day, up to $50 per billing cycle. Shorter outages get no credit.
3. Early termination: plans run on 24-month contracts. Cancelling early costs $200, reduced by $8.33 for each completed month.
4. Bereavement: the early termination fee is waived when the account holder has died, if a death certificate is provided within 60 days.
5. Roaming: usage abroad costs $10 per day used. Roaming charges are final.
6. Unused data: unused data is not refunded and does not roll over.
7. Price lock: a plan's price is fixed for 24 months from activation. After that, prices may change with 30 days' notice.
8. Price matching: Larkspur does not match competitors' prices.
9. Any refund, credit or exception not described above needs approval from a human supervisor. The assistant cannot approve it.
